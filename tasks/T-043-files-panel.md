# T-043: Files panel

The window-level files panel (`files.toggle`, `Ctrl+Shift+B`): opens on
the active pane's target, its header switches between Local and any
saved SSH host, each target keeps its session, and it sits on the
`files_panel` side next to the SFTP sidebar. Includes the setting on the
settings screen and in `src/default-config.yaml`. Area: src.
