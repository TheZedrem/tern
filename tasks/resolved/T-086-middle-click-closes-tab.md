# T-086: Close a tab with a middle click

A middle click on a tab, in the top or bottom bar or in the side tab
list, closes it as its close button does, asking first when SFTP edits
would go. It acts on release over the same tab, never activates the tab
first, and does nothing elsewhere in the bar; in a pane the middle
button stays the terminal's. Requested by the owner (2026-11-12).
